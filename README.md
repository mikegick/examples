# 🇮🇹 Northern Italy Master Itinerary & Travel Guide
**Dates:** April 18–27, 2026 | **Bases:** Milan, Florence, Verona | **Transit:** Rail Only | **Hotel Budget:** $2,000

---

## 🗺️ Master Routing Schedule

### Base 1: Milan (Arrival Hub)
*   **April 18: Touchdown in Italy**
    *   Land at Milan Malpensa (MXP). Catch the 50-minute Malpensa Express train straight to Milano Centrale. Check into your hotel. Spend a relaxed evening exploring the charming, artistic Brera neighborhood for an outdoor aperitivo.
*   **April 19: Iconic Milan Sights**
    *   Explore the magnificent Duomo di Milano and take the lift up to the rooftop terraces to walk among the spires. Stroll through the glass-domed Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II toward Sforza Castle.

### Base 2: Florence (The Tuscan Art Hub)
*   **April 20: High-Speed South to Tuscany**
    *   Board a morning high-speed train from Milano Centrale to Firenze Santa Maria Novella (under 2 hours). Check into your historic center hotel, cross the iconic Ponte Vecchio, and walk up to Piazzale Michelangelo at dusk for the city's best panoramic sunset view.
*   **April 21: Masterpieces & Markets**
    *   See Michelangelo’s David at the Accademia and explore the legendary Renaissance collections at the Uffizi Gallery. Enjoy a relaxed dinner in the quieter Oltrarno district across the river.
*   **April 22: Tuscan Countryside Day Trip**
    *   Take a quick 1-hour and 15-minute regional train to Siena. Wander the massive, medieval Piazza del Campo and enjoy a fresh, garlic-light Tuscan pasta dish (pici tossed in fresh herbs and olive oil).
